Privacy Policy

Develac respects your privacy. We are committed to protecting your data and ensuring secure processing of your documents.

Last updated: 2026

1. Introduction

Develac provides fast, secure and privacy-focused online PDF tools. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our website.

2. Information We Collect

Currently, Develac does not require user registration.

We do NOT collect:
  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Uploaded document content

We may collect anonymous usage data such as browser type, device type, and general interaction statistics to improve our services.

3. File Processing & Deletion

Files uploaded to Develac are processed automatically.

  • Files are stored temporarily for processing only.
  • Files are deleted automatically after processing.
  • We do not access or review document content.
  • Your files are never shared with third parties.

4. Cookies & Analytics

We may use cookies and analytics tools (such as Google Analytics) to understand website performance and improve user experience.

These tools collect anonymous data and do not access your uploaded documents.

5. Advertising (Future)

In the future, Develac may display advertisements to support free access to tools.

Advertising partners will not have access to your files or document content.

6. Security

We use secure HTTPS connections and automated deletion systems to protect your files. While no system is 100% secure, we continuously work to maintain strong protection standards.

7. Future Accounts & Paid Plans

If Develac introduces user accounts or paid plans, additional information such as email address may be required. This policy will be updated accordingly.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Continued use of Develac means you accept any changes.

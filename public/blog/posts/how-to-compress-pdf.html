How to Compress PDF Without Losing Quality

PDF files are widely used for documents, resumes, reports, and invoices. Large file sizes can make sharing difficult. In this guide, you'll learn how to reduce PDF size safely without affecting quality.

Why PDF Files Become Large

Large PDF sizes usually occur because of high-quality embedded elements. Understanding the causes helps you optimize better.

  • High-resolution images
  • Scanned documents stored as images
  • Embedded fonts and graphics
  • Unoptimized layouts and metadata

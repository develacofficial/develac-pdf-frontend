Privacy Policy

Temporary File Processing

All PDF files uploaded to Develac PDF are processed temporarily in memory. Files are used only for compression and are never written to permanent storage.

Automatic File Deletion

Once compression is complete and the file is downloaded, both the original and compressed files are automatically deleted from our servers. No copies are retained.

No Personal Data Storage

We do not collect, store, or process any personal information. No user accounts are required, and we do not track individual users.

No Content Tracking

The content of your PDF files is never analyzed, read, or tracked. Files are not used for analytics, training, or any purpose other than compression.
